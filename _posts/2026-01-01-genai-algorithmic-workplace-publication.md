# 🎉 New Publication Alert!

I'm thrilled to announce that my latest research article has been published in the **Journal of Open Innovation: Technology, Market, and Complexity** (Elsevier).

## Generative AI and the Algorithmic Workplace

This study represents months of rigorous bibliometric and conceptual analysis, investigating how **generative artificial intelligence** is fundamentally transforming the way organisations operate, make decisions, and design work.

### What We Investigated

While there's been considerable research on task automation and human–machine collaboration, we noticed a significant gap: the broader **structural and epistemic implications** of GenAI on:

- **Authority** in organisational settings
- **Coordination mechanisms** between humans and AI
- **Decision-making processes** at all levels

### Our Approach

We conducted a comprehensive bibliometric analysis of **212 Scopus-indexed publications** spanning from 2018 to 2025. Using VOSviewer and Bibliometrix, we mapped:

- Performance trends in the field
- Thematic structures and clusters
- The conceptual evolution of GenAI in workplace contexts

### Key Discoveries

The research reveals a **dynamic and rapidly evolving knowledge domain**. We found fascinating intersections between:

- **Technical constructs** — large language models, generative adversarial networks
- **Behavioural concepts** — human–machine collaboration, trust, skill adaptation
- **Managerial frameworks** — authority structures, coordination, strategic decision-making

### Why This Matters

As organisations increasingly integrate GenAI into their operations, understanding these dynamics becomes crucial. This isn't just about efficiency gains—it's about **rethinking the fundamental architecture of work** in the age of intelligent systems.
